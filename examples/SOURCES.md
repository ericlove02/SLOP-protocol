Regenerate the procedural images with:

```bash
python scripts/make_examples.py
```

and re-fetch the two external images with `--fetch-external` (needs `scikit-image`
and network access to `raw.githubusercontent.com`).

Images in `examples/outputs/` were produced by the image-generation models named
in `benchmarks/results/` from SLOP packets of the originals above.
